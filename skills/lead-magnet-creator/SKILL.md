# Lead Magnet Creator

Turns a topic or offer idea into a complete, structured lead magnet asset — the actual document someone receives after opting in, not a pitch for one.

## When to use

Use this when a lead magnet needs to exist as a deliverable: a guide, checklist, template, swipe file, or worksheet that will sit behind an opt-in form. It builds the asset itself. It does not write the landing page copy, the opt-in form, or the emails that follow a download — those are separate jobs. Once the asset exists, [[lead-magnet-engager]] picks up from there and drafts the nurture sequence that follows the download.

## Inputs needed

Before drafting, confirm:
- **The idea or topic** — what problem this lead magnet solves or what outcome it delivers
- **Asset type** — guide, checklist, template, swipe file, worksheet, or cheat sheet (if undecided, recommend one based on the topic and say why)
- **Audience/ICP** — who this is for and what they already know, so the asset is pitched at the right level
- **Depth constraint** — a rough length or scope ceiling; a lead magnet that takes an hour to consume defeats the purpose

If the audience or asset type is missing, ask rather than guess — the wrong format for the topic (e.g., a checklist for something that needs worked examples) undermines the whole asset.

## Asset structure conventions

Match structure to type. Every asset, regardless of type, opens with a one-line statement of the specific outcome the reader gets from using it — not the topic, the outcome.

- **Checklist** — numbered, single-action items, each one independently completable and checkable off. No item should bundle two actions.
- **Template** — a fill-in-the-blank structure with one worked example filled in above the blank version, so the reader sees exactly what "done" looks like.
- **Guide** — short sections with a clear takeaway per section, ordered so each section is usable on its own if the reader skips ahead.
- **Swipe file** — a curated set of labeled examples, each with a one-line note on why it works, not just a raw dump of samples.
- **Worksheet** — prompts or questions with visible space/structure for the reader's own answers, not prose to read passively.

Rules that apply across all types:
- Every claim or step must be immediately actionable — cut anything that's context-setting without a next action attached.
- Keep it finishable in one sitting. If the topic is too big for that, narrow the scope rather than lengthen the asset.
- No placeholder content — if an input needed to complete a section is missing, flag the gap instead of inventing filler.

## Output format

The complete lead magnet asset, formatted per its type above, ready to hand to design/production with no placeholder text. Precede it with one line naming the asset type chosen and the specific outcome it delivers, so that framing carries into the opt-in page copy downstream.
