# Idea Review

Scores one raw content idea against a fit-and-impact bar and returns a clear verdict — proceed to brief, rework, or reject — before any writing time is spent on it.

## When to use

Use this at the front of the content pipeline, before a brief gets written or ideas go head-to-head against each other. It exists to stop weak ideas early, not to polish good ones — leave voice and structure notes for later pipeline stages.

## Inputs needed

Before scoring, confirm you have:
- **The idea itself** — topic, angle, or hook, in one or two sentences
- **Target channel** — blog, LinkedIn, X, YouTube, newsletter, etc.
- **Audience/ICP** — who this is for, if not obvious from house context

If any of these are missing, ask rather than guess — a fit score without a channel or audience is meaningless.

## Scoring rubric

Score the idea on two independent axes, 1–5 each.

### Fit (1–5)
How well does this idea match what this account/brand is known for and who follows it?
- **5** — Squarely inside an existing content pillar; audience has asked for or engaged with this exact topic before.
- **3** — Adjacent to existing pillars; plausible but not an obvious extension.
- **1** — Off-brand, off-audience, or requires explaining why it's relevant at all.

### Impact (1–5)
If this idea is executed well, how much does it move the needle?
- **5** — Ties directly to a current goal (launch, campaign, proof point), has a clear differentiated angle, and timing is right.
- **3** — Solid, useful content with no particular urgency or differentiation.
- **1** — Generic, saying nothing that hasn't been said, or the timing has already passed.

## Verdict bar

Combined score = Fit + Impact (max 10).

- **8–10 → Proceed to brief.** State the combined score and one sentence on why it clears the bar.
- **5–7 → Rework.** Name the weaker axis and what would need to change to clear the bar — a sharper angle, a different channel, tighter ICP fit.
- **2–4 → Reject.** Say so plainly, with the reason. Don't soften a clear reject into a rework.

Never round a borderline score up to avoid a hard conversation — a 7 is a rework, not a pass.

## Output format

Return, in this order:
1. Fit score + one-line reasoning
2. Impact score + one-line reasoning
3. Combined score
4. Verdict (Proceed / Rework / Reject) with the one actionable reason behind it

Keep it short — this is a gate, not a critique. Save deeper notes for the brief stage once an idea has passed.
