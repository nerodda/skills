# Launch Video

Turns a launch's narrative — what's shipping, why it matters, the proof — into a structured video outline: hook, beats, and CTA. It decides the shape of the story, not the exact words spoken on camera.

## When to use

Use at the very start of the video pipeline, only for videos tied to a specific launch event: a product release, feature ship, campaign kickoff, or timed announcement. This is an outline stage — it produces a narrative structure, not a script. Once the outline is approved, hand it to [[youtube-script]] to write the full shot-by-shot script. Pull proof points from [[ship-scan]] (what actually shipped) or [[results-scan]] (what the numbers show) before outlining — a launch video without verified proof points is just marketing copy with a camera pointed at it.

If the video isn't tied to a launch (an evergreen tutorial, a repurposed post, a general explainer), this isn't the right skill — go straight to [[youtube-script]] with a brief, or route through [[long-form]] / [[repurpose]] instead.

## Inputs needed

- **The launch** — what's shipping, the ship date, the one-sentence headline claim
- **Proof points** — metrics, screenshots, before/after, customer quotes (from [[ship-scan]] / [[results-scan]] if available; don't invent numbers)
- **Target length and format** — 60-second teaser, 3–5 minute explainer, or long-form walkthrough
- **Audience** — existing users, prospects, or press/investors; the hook and stakes differ by audience
- **Production constraints** — what footage exists vs. needs to be shot, on-camera talent available, launch date deadline

## Outline structure

Every launch video outline has five beats. Don't skip or reorder them:

1. **Hook (0–5s)** — the single most surprising or high-stakes claim, stated cold, before any logo or intro. No throat-clearing.
2. **Context** — the problem this launch solves, stated in terms the audience already feels, not in terms of the product.
3. **The reveal** — what's actually shipping, shown or stated plainly, tied directly back to the context beat.
4. **Proof** — the strongest available evidence this works: a number, a demo, a before/after, a quote. If there's no real proof point yet, say so rather than outlining around a placeholder.
5. **CTA** — one specific next action (not "learn more" — the actual link, signup, or command).

Pacing rule: for anything under 90 seconds, context and reveal can compress into one beat. For anything over 3 minutes, proof can expand into multiple sub-beats (one per proof point), each with its own mini hook.

## Output format

Return:
1. **Outline** — numbered beats with a rough timecode range per beat and one line describing what happens in it
2. **Hook options** — 2–3 alternative opening lines, each usable standalone
3. **CTA options** — 1–2 alternative closing actions if more than one makes sense
4. **Open questions** — any proof point, footage, or claim that needs confirming before this goes to script

Do not write dialogue or shot directions — that's [[youtube-script]]'s job once this outline is approved.
